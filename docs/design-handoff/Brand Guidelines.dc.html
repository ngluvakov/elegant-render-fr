Elegant Render brand guidelines

This is the English text version of the brand guideline handoff. The canonical implementation notes and final homepage copy live in docs/design-handoff/README.md.

Brand position

Elegant Render is an international architectural visualization service by White Rook DOO. It should feel precise, useful, modern, and easy to buy from.

Visual system

ElementGuideline
CanvasWhite or very light neutral backgrounds.
TextBlack or near-black, with restrained hierarchy.
AccentGreen #00D98A for decisive interaction and focus.
TypographyInter Tight for interface and editorial copy; JetBrains Mono for compact technical labels.
ShapeCompact radii, clear grid, no decorative blobs.
ImageryRealistic renders and service outcomes. Avoid abstract placeholders.

Voice

Use sentence case, calm practical wording, clear prices, and short explanations. Avoid hype and exclamation marks.

Conversion

The page should help the visitor understand the service, see proof, review pricing anchors, and start an estimate without needing a sales call.
