# Lovart image briefs - interior render

Use these briefs for interior render service pages and cards.

## Main image

Create a photorealistic interior render of a modern apartment living room. Use natural daylight, balanced neutral tones, warm wood, soft textiles, and a clear view of furniture layout. The room should feel ready to live in and easy to understand.

## Detail image

Create a close-up of realistic interior materials: fabric sofa, wood floor, stone surface, soft curtain, and gentle shadow. Make the render quality visible through texture and light.

## Process image

Show an interior render workflow with a reference mood board, simple plan, and final render preview on a clean desk. Keep it editorial and uncluttered.

## Avoid

- Overly luxury styling.
- Unrealistic glossy surfaces.
- Extreme wide-angle distortion.
- People as the focus.
