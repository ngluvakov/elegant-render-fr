# Serbian platform decision archive

This file is the English archive pointer for the original Serbian platform decision log. The full pre-fork wording remains available in git history. It is intentionally not carried forward as active guidance for elegantrender.com because the international fork has different routes, payment processing, currency rules, legal pages, and launch gates.

## What survives into the international fork

- The portal and order lifecycle remain the operational base.
- Auth, CRM sync, file upload, admin review, revision, and deliverable concepts remain in place.
- The platform keeps a lightweight decision log in `docs/platform-decisions.md`.
- Any future change to conversion, pricing, brand/design rules, order lifecycle, auth, payments, CRM sync, or architecture must be logged there.

## What is superseded

- Serbian public routes are superseded by English routes.
- The old local card-payment design is superseded by PayPal.
- Local-only currency and tax copy is superseded by EUR accounting and international legal copy.
- The old warm regional brand book is superseded by the White Rook international design handoff.

Use this archive as historical context only. Active implementation guidance lives in `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/plan/**`, and `docs/design-handoff/**`.
