# Lovart image briefs - day-to-dusk

Use these briefs for day-to-dusk transformation imagery.

## Main before/after

Create a matched before/after exterior property image. The left side is a clean daylight scene. The right side is the same view at dusk with warm interior lights, balanced sky color, and visible architectural detail. Camera, geometry, and landscaping must match.

## Detail image

Create a close-up of windows and facade lighting at dusk. Keep reflections believable and avoid over-saturated sky colors.

## Ad image

Create a real estate listing-style exterior that feels more inviting after a day-to-dusk edit. Leave negative space for ad copy outside the image.

## Avoid

- Fully night scenes.
- Neon colors.
- Unrealistic glowing windows.
- Changing the property design.
