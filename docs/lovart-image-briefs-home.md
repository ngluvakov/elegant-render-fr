# Lovart image briefs - home page

Use these briefs for homepage imagery on elegantrender.com. The images should show real spatial outcomes: finished rooms, exteriors, plans, and before/after transformations. Avoid abstract decoration.

## Direction

- Bright white or neutral setting with realistic materials.
- Elegant but approachable residential and small-development spaces.
- Clear architectural geometry.
- Natural light, soft shadows, and precise details.
- Green accent may appear as a tiny UI or styling detail, never as a dominant wash.

## Hero image

Create a refined architectural render scene for an international 3D visualization studio. Show a modern living room with warm natural light, tactile materials, realistic furniture, and a calm editorial composition. The image should feel premium but accessible, with enough empty space for headline text on the left.

## Pricing anchor image

Create a clean comparison composition showing three service cards as real visual outcomes: an interior render, an exterior render, and a 360 virtual tour preview. The composition should feel like product photography for digital services, not an app screenshot.

## Process image

Create a four-step visual sequence: files received, first drafts, revision round, final render delivery. Use subtle workspace objects, plans, render previews, and download cues. Keep it uncluttered and realistic.

## Before/after image

Create a split-view room transformation: empty room on one side, virtually staged finished room on the other. Match camera position and lighting. The furnished side should feel believable and lived-in, not showroom-perfect.

## Avoid

- Dark cinematic scenes.
- Luxury mansion cliches.
- Heavy gradients or floating abstract shapes.
- People as the primary subject.
- Text embedded inside the image.
