# Pillar 1 pricing extract

This file records the English EUR pricing baseline for the international Elegant Render launch. Track A owns executable catalog prices and calculation rules. Track B may translate catalog labels only after the S2 handoff.

## Public anchor prices

| Service family | Launch anchor |
| --- | ---: |
| Interior render | From €169 |
| Exterior render | From €249 |
| 360 virtual tour | From €294 |

These x9 prices are the public anchor points for international marketing copy. The executable catalog may contain more granular service, quantity, and add-on rules.

## Copy rules

- Use `estimate`, not `quote`, in user-facing UI.
- Use `starting at` only where the scope can change by quantity, view, room, duration, or output.
- Explain that invoices are issued in EUR.
- Do not add tax claims outside the payment/legal owner tracks.
- Do not translate frozen catalog IDs or slugs.

## Catalog handoff

Track A finalizes:

- Base prices.
- Included quantities.
- Extra unit prices.
- Duration multipliers.
- Currency display conversion.
- Accounting and invoice behavior.

Track B receives the copy surface after S2:

- Service labels.
- Short descriptions.
- Included-item labels.
- Empty states and validation copy.
