# Lovart image briefs - photomontage

Use these briefs for photomontage imagery.

## Main image

Create a realistic photomontage showing a proposed modern house integrated into a real street photograph. The render should match camera angle, lens, sun direction, shadows, and material exposure.

## Process image

Show three stages on a clean workspace: site photo, transparent massing overlay, and final integrated render. Avoid readable labels.

## Detail image

Create a close crop where the rendered building meets the real pavement, planting, and neighboring context. Focus on believable shadow contact and scale.

## Avoid

- Obvious cutout edges.
- Mismatched perspective.
- Dramatic weather changes.
- Over-polished building materials that do not fit the photo.
