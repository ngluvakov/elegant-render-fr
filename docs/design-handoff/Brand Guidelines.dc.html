01

Brand relationship

Elegant Render is the client-facing architectural visualization sub-brand of White Rook DOO. On international sites it keeps its own logo and name, while inheriting the White Rook design system in full — colors, typography, spacing, components and motion. The two logos are never combined into a lockup; White Rook appears once, in the footer, as the parent company.

White Rook DOO

Parent company. Owns the design system, the pricing engine, the backend, Bitrix CRM and operations.

Sub-brand

Elegant Render

Market-facing brand for B2C and international audiences. Own logo, White Rook system.

Rules

  • The Elegant Render logo is the primary mark on all Elegant Render properties. The White Rook logo never appears in the header.
  • Footer attribution, always verbatim: "Elegant Render is part of White Rook DOO — 3D visualization & digital assets." paired with the white horizontal White Rook logo.
  • Do not place both logos at the same hierarchy level, and do not merge them into one lockup.
  • Content, pages, pricing logic, images, backend and CRM stay identical to the Serbian platform — only the skin defined in this document changes.
02

Logo

The Elegant Render mark is intentionally classical — a serif "E" monogram and all-caps wordmark inside a double-rule frame. It is the one deliberate contrast in the system: a heritage face on a precise, modern body. Do not "modernize" the logo; let the system around it do that work.

Usage

  • Header size: 44–48px height, paired with the name "Elegant Render" set in Inter Tight Medium 15px.
  • Minimum height 32px in UI; clear space equal to the frame's rule spacing on all sides.
  • Only pure black #111111 or pure white — never green, never grey, never on photography without a protection gradient.
  • No shadows, gradients, outlines, rotation or stretching. Use the provided PNG/SVG assets; never redraw.
03

Color

The warm ivory/clay/sage palette of the Serbian site is fully retired internationally. The system is monochrome-first: white canvas, near-black text, grey structure — and one green. Imagery carries all the warmth; the UI stays out of its way.

04

Typography

Cormorant Garamond and Manrope are retired. Two families do all the work: Inter Tight for display and body, JetBrains Mono for eyebrows, specs, prices and file names. The signature pairing — an all-caps mono eyebrow over a sentence-case display headline — replaces the serif/sans contrast of the old identity.

05

Space, surfaces & components

Layout

  • 8px base grid; content max-width 1280px
  • Section padding 128px desktop / 64px tablet / 48px mobile
  • Radius: 4px for buttons, inputs, cards. Images: none. Pills only for status chips
  • Borders 1px #e8e8e8; 2px reserved for focus and selected tabs
  • The old 1rem radius system (rounded-2xl/3xl cards) is retired

Cards

  • White, 1px #e8e8e8 border, no shadow at rest
  • Images bleed flush to the card edge; content padded 24px
  • Hover: border → #d4d4d4 + hairline shadow, 200ms
  • Dark surfaces (#0a0a0a) only for footer, hero protection and one feature panel per page

06

Motion

One easing curve, three durations, fades over slides. Motion confirms an action or reveals content — it never decorates. Everything animated on the Serbian site is either replaced or removed below.

Easing — always

cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)

Durations

120ms — press
200ms — hover, tabs
360ms — drawers, modals, image reveals

Principles

Fades over slides. No springs, no bounce, no parallax. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

07

Imagery

The renders are the brand's warmth — the same portfolio serves both markets, untouched. No filters, no tints, no radius. When text sits on imagery, use a bottom-up protection gradient, never a flat wash or blur.

Residential / aerial / 2026

Protection gradient: rgba(10,10,10,0.6) → transparent

Aspect ratios

16:9 — heroes, walkthroughs
4:3 — architectural stills
3:2 — service & feature cards
1:1 — product / detail shots

Treatment

Shown as delivered — warm interiors stay warm. Card hover may scale the image 1.02 over 360ms. Nothing else.

08

Voice & copy

The international voice is a hybrid: White Rook's spec-sheet clarity with Elegant Render's warmth. Short sentences, verbs up front, numbers numeric — but written for someone furnishing a home, not procuring an asset. Always "we", never "I". Sentence case everywhere. No emoji, no exclamation points, no "stunning".

Western-market notes

  • Lead with the deliverable and the timeframe: "First drafts in 3–5 working days."
  • Prices in € with "from" pricing; keep the model-first savings story front and center — it is the differentiator.
  • TÜV Rheinland / ISO certifications are a primary trust signal in DACH and Western Europe — surface them on every page.
  • "Hand-crafted" replaces "ručna izrada" — it counters the AI-generated assumption without over-claiming.
