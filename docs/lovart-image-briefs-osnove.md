# Lovart image briefs - floor plans

Use these briefs for 2D and 3D floor plan imagery.

## 2D floor plan

Create a clean architectural 2D floor plan on a white canvas. Show room labels as subtle placeholders without readable text, measured walls, furniture outlines, and a restrained black-and-green annotation style. The result should feel precise and client-friendly.

## 3D floor plan

Create an angled 3D floor plan of a compact apartment. Show walls, furniture, flooring, bathroom fixtures, and kitchen cabinetry. Keep the camera high enough to understand layout. Use neutral materials and soft daylight.

## Detail crop

Create a close-up of a plan corner with furniture, door swing, and dimension cues. The crop should show craftsmanship and clarity, not dense technical drafting.

## Avoid

- Crowded CAD screenshots.
- Strong color coding.
- Readable private addresses or names.
- Decorative background patterns.
