## Positioning

Elegant Render is an online-first architectural visualization service for homeowners, real estate teams, architects, small developers, and property marketers who need clear visuals before they commit money, time, or construction work.

The brand promise is simple: clear prices, structured delivery, and carefully made render work. Elegant Render should feel useful and refined, not distant or luxury-coded. It should make buying a render feel understandable.

## Relationship to White Rook

Elegant Render is the public product brand. White Rook DOO is the seller, production company, and operational backbone.

Use this relationship quietly and consistently:

- Footer attribution: `Elegant Render is part of White Rook DOO - 3D visualization and digital assets.`
- Legal pages: White Rook DOO is the contracting party.
- About page: explain the studio relationship in plain language.
- Hero and primary conversion surfaces: lead with Elegant Render.

## Voice

Write like a practical studio lead explaining the process to a smart client.

Use:

- Short sentences.
- Sentence case.
- Direct labels.
- Concrete delivery terms.
- `estimate` in UI copy.
- `working days` for timing.

Avoid:

- Exclamation marks.
- Pressure language.
- Luxury cliches.
- Overclaiming speed or quality.
- Abstract art direction where a practical service label is clearer.

## Visual identity

The international design system is defined in `docs/design-handoff/README.md` and implemented by Track C. Treat that handoff as canonical.

Core rules:

- White or near-white canvas.
- Black text and restrained neutral surfaces.
- Green accent `#00D98A` for decisive interaction moments.
- Inter Tight for the main interface.
- JetBrains Mono for technical microcopy and compact labels.
- Small radii, disciplined spacing, and editorial composition.
- Real project imagery or generated bitmap imagery, not decorative shapes.

## Conversion structure

The first screen should communicate three things quickly:

- What Elegant Render makes.
- Who it helps.
- How pricing starts.

Recommended public flow:

1. Home page with final handoff copy, proof imagery, pricing anchors, service overview, process, FAQ, and CTA.
2. Services index with clear categories.
3. Service detail pages with examples, scope, starting price, delivery, and CTA.
4. Pricing configurator for the actual estimate flow.
5. Checkout as details to PayPal.
6. Post-payment source-file upload in success screen and portal.

## Pricing voice

Pricing should feel transparent, not cheap.

Use:

- `Starting at €169`
- `Your estimate updates as you add views, rooms, or outputs.`
- `Invoices are issued in EUR.`
- `You can see a local display price where supported.`

Avoid:

- `Cheap renders`
- `Request a quote` in UI copy
- Any claim that the platform charges a different accounting currency than EUR
