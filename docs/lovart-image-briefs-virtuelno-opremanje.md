# Lovart image briefs - virtual staging

Use these briefs for virtual staging imagery.

## Main before/after

Create a matched before/after image of an empty apartment living room. The after side adds realistic furniture, rug, lighting, art, and plants while preserving the room geometry and daylight.

## Style variants

Create three virtual staging styles for the same room: calm modern, warm Scandinavian, and refined urban. Keep camera angle and architecture unchanged.

## Detail image

Create a close-up of staged furniture and decor that shows believable scale, shadows, and contact with the floor.

## Avoid

- Blocking windows or important room features.
- Furniture that is too large for the room.
- Over-decorated spaces.
- Changing wall positions or floor plan.
